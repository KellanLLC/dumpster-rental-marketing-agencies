# Dumpster rental marketing agencies: published prices and services (US, October 2026)

A dated table of 35 US marketing agencies and software companies that sell to dumpster rental and roll-off companies: what each one says it does, the price it publishes, whether it runs ads, and whether it builds online booking. Every row was read off the company's own website between October 18 and October 31, 2026.

## Conflict of interest

This table was compiled by Boston Thueson, founder of [Pull Count](https://pullcount.com), a dumpster rental marketing agency that appears in it and competes with the other companies listed. Prices are only what each company publishes; "not published" means the site asks you to book a call. Nothing here was paid for.

## How it was collected

- Sources: each company's own pricing, services and home pages. No third-party estimates.
- `published_price` is copied as written, including setup fees and ad spend notes.
- `runs_ads`: the company sells Google Ads or Local Services Ads management.
- `online_booking`: the company builds websites where a customer can pick a bin size and date and book online.
- `date_read` is the day the row was read. Prices change, so check the date before you quote one.
